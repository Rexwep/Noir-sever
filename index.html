Studio rules

Original work.
Clear terms.

No reused complete designs. Scope, responsibilities and boundaries are written before work begins.

01

No design is remade.

This applies from Basic to Signature. Two projects may share a category. They will not share a look.

02

Client direction. Studio structure.

The client sets colour, logomark, tone and primary action. The studio locks structure, typography, composition and page count.

03

One revision follows lock.

Additional pages or features are quoted separately. Client delay pauses the delivery clock.

04

Signature Model.

A Signature project is registered to the client by name. A model number is issued. An official card appears in the administration area after login. That number and design are retired.

05

Care is maintenance.

Website Care maintains the live site. It is not a redesign. New pages, features, commerce builds and major SEO campaigns are separate work.

06

Published scope is the offer.

Domain and hosting follow the package list. Search visibility is prepared; first-place ranking is not promised.
